## English

`SolarRider_Board_20261012.epro2` is the EasyEDA Pro project. It has two PCB pages. The older one, with 16-pin headers on each side for the ESP32, is the board in the photos. The Gerbers I ordered are in [PCB1_20260626_Gerbers.zip](fabrication/PCB1_20260626_Gerbers.zip): 13 Gerber layers and two drill files from June 2026.

How the modules connect is in the [power guide](../../docs/en/power-wiring.md). The 4056-TP output goes into the XR2981.
